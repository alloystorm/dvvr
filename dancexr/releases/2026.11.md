# DanceXR 2026.11 Release Notes

DanceXR 2026.11 turns Native from a viewer into a place where you can pose, paint, and film characters. Grab a limb and pose any actor over any motion, draw on a character's body, spray water at it, or step into the scene as a player avatar. Native and the Unity apps now load FBX and VRM models, and a new Creator edition adds offline video recording at any resolution. Actors get a right-click quick menu, models swap with a particle transition, and the path-traced and raster renderers both look cleaner.

## What is New

### Pose tool

- Drag a hand, foot, or the body of **any actor, over any motion**, to pose it. The actor detaches from its dance at the pose it is in, and the others keep playing.
- A balance solver keeps the character standing: weight shifts between the feet, a lifted foot stays in the air, and legs bend or the body sits when you pull it down.
- **Resume** blends the actor back into its motion at the current time. **Revert** undoes your edits.
- Works in VR with the controllers. The pose tool is free in every edition.

### Body paint and spray (Pro)

- **Body paint** draws directly on a character's skin and clothing. Choose Point, Stroke, Line, or Polygon drawing, erase, fill, and use a stamp image as the brush.
- Brush size, stamp rotation, and draw mode are adjusted with the mouse wheel or VR stick. Painted color can optionally be saved with the model.
- The **Spray tool** shoots water at a character. Bind two controls to launch speed, spread, or quantity, and set up **Shower** or **Fountain** spawn presets that you can save.
- Water now **washes paint off** and carries its color down the body, leaving stains on clean skin and tinting the droplets. How fast paint washes off is adjustable.
- A global **tool menu** switches between Select, Paint, Spray, and Pose, and each tool owns the wheel and the middle click while it is active.

### Player avatar (Pro)

- Stand in the scene as a **player avatar**: a male upper-body dummy by default, or any model you pick, including one inside a zip pack.
- Choose standing or seated, with a chair to sit on. Lean, weight shift, arm and leg positions are adjustable, and the avatar can walk.
- A **third-person over-the-shoulder view** on desktop, with eye contact, height snapping, and a camera fade that lets you see through the body when the camera is near the head.
- Hair, clothing, softbody, and PMX physics now react to the avatar's body, and to your VR hands and fingers. System > Physics has **Collide with Player** and **Collide with Other Actors** switches.
- Use the Pose tool on the avatar's arms to place its hands.

### Stockings (Pro)

- Add **sheer stockings** to any character. A knit is shaded over the skin and stretches as the body moves, so it thins over bent knees and thickens where the skin relaxes.
- Control the height and angle of the hem, with a welt band and a soft or hard edge.
- Works in both the path tracer and the raster renderer, with built-in presets and your own.

### FBX and VRM models

- **FBX and VRM characters load in Native** in every edition. Bones are mapped automatically, hair and outfit parts get physics, and textures are found even when the exporter's paths are wrong.
- VRM models show their license terms in the actor menu so you know how a model may be used.
- Models that ship as a zip with several variants now show a **preview** before you choose, and each model in a pack is tracked as its own recent and favorite entry.

### Quick menu and actor control

- **Right-click a character** for a quick menu: assign a motion, choose a variant or alternative textures, show or hide dressing groups, frame the character in the camera, face the camera, reset position or physics, or unload. Right-click empty space for tool, playback, and camera shortcuts.
- **Alternative texture sets** from a model pack can be switched from the menu or the quick menu, and are saved with the model.
- **Replacing an actor** now swaps the selected actor and keeps its slot.
- Characters can **breathe**, and **eyelid expression morphs** such as blinks and winks work on XPS models. Eye contact and the idle pose have been refined.
- Characters now **dissolve into flakes** when they are replaced or removed, and form out of flakes when they arrive. The rest of the cast **slides** to its new place instead of jumping. A spinner on the floor shows where the next character will appear.

### Cloth and hair

- Cloth holds up better: contacts are resolved inside the solver, garments can use **long-range attachments** to stop stretching, and **self-collision** can be turned on. Skirts can be built as strings, and the ties between strands are adjustable.
- **Lateral links** now detect where a skirt has slits and can be tuned to hold shape or move freely.
- Hair chains hang from the correct pivot, and ponytails on long, uneven bones sit properly instead of tipping over.
- Friction between a body and hair or cloth no longer pushes strands into the body.

### Offline video recording (Creator)

- **Record video** renders a clip at any resolution and frame rate, independent of how fast your PC draws it, and writes MP4 or a PNG sequence with your music mixed in.
- A script of camera keyframes or an orbit around an actor can drive the shot, and recordings leave out on-screen handles and cursors.
- The recording menu is available in the Creator edition.

### Lighting and rendering

- Raster mode gains **probe-volume global illumination** and improved ray-traced GI, with less ghosting.
- The path tracer now respects **Double sided** on materials, and its shadows are culled more efficiently.
- New **sky presets** (Morning, Noon, Sunset, Night) and separate sky and sun lighting controls.
- Subsurface scattering, skin tone, and several raster outline and lighting artifacts are fixed.
- Fog no longer glows from ambient light, and it renders correctly on skin and in the mirror.
- The room menu is reorganized.
- The first-launch graphics check has been reworked.

## Quality-of-Life Improvements

- Menus use Title Case throughout and have been reviewed and tidied.
- The UI is larger, with more spacing, and text entry has an on-screen keypad.
- A loading entry in the message list shows what Native is preparing.
- Touch input works on touch screens.
- Playback speed no longer jumps after a frame-rate dip.
- VR improvements to the menu and avatar motion.

## Licensing

- A DanceXR Native license is now good for **one release**. When a new version is released, one-time purchases carry forward automatically after a silent check; Patreon and monthly pledges cover the release they were made for.
- One licence activation can now cover several editions, and Native picks the highest tier you own.

## DanceXR HD, LW, and RT

- **FBX characters now load through the same reader as Native**, replacing the older loader. Textures, materials, and morphs are handled more consistently, including Daz and Unreal exports.
- **VRM characters** can be dropped in or loaded from a zip, and show their license in the actor menu.
- FBX and VRM models get **automatic bone mapping**, so they can dance without manual setup.
- Hair, skirt, and accessory chains are detected on more models, including ones where strands are numbered without spaces and ones named by what hangs from them.
- Hair chains hang from the correct pivot and turn with their own segments, so ponytails on long bones no longer tip or lag a segment behind.
- Friction against the body no longer drives strands into it.
- Fixed box shapes converting to tablets incorrectly in the physics editor.
